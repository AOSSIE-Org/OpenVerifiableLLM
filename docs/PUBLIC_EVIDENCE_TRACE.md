# Public evidence status

Production training and final model releases have not run. Existing development
checks do not establish completion of the full training trajectory.

The complete English Wikipedia and selected public conversation data were acquired
and prepared. A separate clean reconstruction reproduced all recorded data outputs.
The existing RTX 5090 pilot completed record, complete pilot replay and resume checks,
plus sustained Wikipedia and conversation pilot replays. These are operator-run
checks, not independent third-party verification or full production replay.

Historical evidence is undergoing a privacy-preserving publication migration.
Unchanged numerical inputs, tensors and transformation outputs are retained, but
public acceptance through affected historical references remains suspended until
the replacement source snapshots, inventories and signatures are published and
checked by fresh download. A sanitized report has its own digest and cannot stand
in for the bytes of a withdrawn signed record.

See [the technical verification requirements](VERIFIABLE_WIKIPEDIA_PLAN.md),
[the complete verifier](COMPLETE_VERIFIER.md), and
[the publication boundary](PUBLICATION_BOUNDARY.md). Model provenance and
generated-answer factual accuracy are separate claims.
