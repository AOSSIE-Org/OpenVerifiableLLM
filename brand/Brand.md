# OpenVerifiableLLM brand

## Logo

The mark is a speech bubble (the model talks) holding a check mark (you can check it).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| [`logo.svg`](logo.svg) | Main logo, any size. Transparent background |
| [`favicon.svg`](favicon.svg) | Browser tab and app icon |
| [`../docs/images/openverifiablellm_logo.png`](../docs/images/openverifiablellm_logo.png) | Raster logo (512 × 512) used in the README |

Keep clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of its width. Don't recolour, stretch, rotate or
add effects. The logo works as is on white and on dark backgrounds. As text, the green has enough contrast on
white and the yellow only on dark backgrounds.

The name is written **OpenVerifiableLLM**, one word, with that capitalisation. Short form: **OVL**.

## Colours

The two logo colours are AOSSIE's green and yellow. Use them for the logo and for small accents only;
text and backgrounds stay neutral.

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Logo, primary accent | Verified green | `#008542` |
| Logo, secondary accent | Check yellow | `#FFCC00` |
| Success on dark backgrounds | Signal green | `#3DDC84` |
| Dark background | Ink | `#0F1A14` |
| Dark surface | Ink raised | `#17291F` |
| Border on dark | Ink line | `#3A5446` |
| Text on dark | Mist | `#D7E3DC` |
| Muted text on dark | Moss | `#7F9A8B` |

On light backgrounds, use the platform's neutral text colours (for example GitHub's) with the two logo
colours as accents. Don't put yellow text on white: it fails contrast.

## Typography

| Use | Typeface |
|---|---|
| Body and headings | The system UI sans-serif: `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif` |
| Commands, hashes, terminal snippets | `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, "DejaVu Sans Mono", monospace` |

Hashes are always set in monospace and may be shortened with an ellipsis (`8de1d600…`) when the full
value is linked or shown nearby.

## Voice

Plain and checkable. Say what was measured and how someone can check it; prefer a command over an
adjective. Example: "every one of its 37 training segments recomputed bit for bit", not "fully
trustworthy".
