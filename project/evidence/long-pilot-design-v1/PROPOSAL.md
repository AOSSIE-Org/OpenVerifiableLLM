# Next sustained development pilot — design, not executed evidence

Dependencies: successful actual tiny CUDA record/replay; completed full prepared
Wikipedia/conversation streams; fresh singleton/rate/budget checks. Production
registration and updates remain forbidden until all parent/forecast gates pass.

The candidate CPU measurement shows 148,046,771 initial-state bytes and
333,138,942 complete state bytes after one update. One audited CUDA process in v3
exported53,463,122 bytes including its generated bytecode cache in34 observed
seconds. These are actual planning inputs, not a representative GPU speed result.

A600second record plus complete fresh-process replay needs separate activity
identities, exact recorded-parent binding and enough time to export both sets of
complete checkpoints. Do not stretch the existing finite v3 plan's absolute1500s
compute/start+1800s export admission or reuse a fake rental start. Keep that
existing admission unchanged. A separately versioned development dispatcher must
retain the same fixed rental deadline,300s useful-progress and1800s durable-export

polling metadata cannot renew useful progress.

Before a later stage starts, the previous declared outputs must be exported and
fully verified. Select replay's record digest from the actual off-pod record,
then durably record its derived job descriptor before launch. On restart,
reconstruct that derivation from retained bytes; do not create another record job
or change any stage/rental deadline. Bind every stage to the same physical pod,
audited code/runtime, exact stream and model/kernel recipe. A failed or stopped
stage prevents its successors and triggers complete preservation and teardown.

sizes. The default interval128 may create far too many333MB states for a short
operational rental. A record with only initial/final checkpoints can supply a full
replay but cannot supply a noninitial/nonfinal resume test. Keep that distinction;
use an actually measured representative schedule before the sustained forecast.
Both Wikipedia and conversation pilots must use their complete prepared-stream
parent manifests as required by production_parents.py. Development cycling never
counts as production coverage.

The existing versioned exporter can rehash and reuse identical immutable content
objects across snapshots; this may avoid retransmitting identical recorded/replay
state bytes or repeated bytecode. Do not assume savings before measuring them, and
retain a complete logical inventory for every snapshot. Budget worst-case bytes,
full hashing, public publication/reverification, remaining reconstruction/replay,
termination grace and billing slack. One GPU and the$100 cap/$10 reserve remain
